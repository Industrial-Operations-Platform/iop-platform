# Product vision

IOP helps industrial teams understand operations, coordinate work and find the
assets and context needed to act. It is reusable across customers and sites,
with a generic domain and configurable local vocabulary and processes.

The platform connects workforce and shifts, handovers, maintenance and assets
with operational intelligence. OIP is its Operational Intelligence module,
retaining the historical focus on event analytics and data quality.

Success means operators can locate an asset, understand a recurring event and
carry its context into a handover or maintenance record without reconciling
unrelated identifiers manually. Supervisors can trace insights to their sources.

Principles: explicit module ownership, customer isolation, traceable normalized
data, auditable changes and read-only access to industrial source systems.
Pilot success measures and numeric targets remain to be agreed before delivery.
